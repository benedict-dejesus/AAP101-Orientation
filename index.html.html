AAP 101 · Art Appreciation

Course description

A three-unit general elective that develops your ability to appreciate, analyze and critique works of art. You'll explore the nature, assumptions and functions of art; artists and artisans; major art movements; the visual elements and principles of design; and Philippine art and its National Artists.

You'll judge artworks with Feldman's method, watch a live theater performance, and apply the basics of coffee painting, photography, videography and guitar.

Aligned with the Philippine Qualifications Framework Level 6.

AAP 101 · Course Syllabus · Grading System19
Attendance · 10% of each term grade

18 points, one for each week

How it's scored

Attendance = (18 − deductions) ÷ 18 × 100
Once a week (one 3-hour session)
−1 per absence
Twice a week (two 1.5-hour sessions)
−0.5 per absence

Example, once a week with 2 absences: (18 − 2) ÷ 18 × 100 = 88.89.

Allowable absences for the semester

3

for classes that meet once a week

6

for classes that meet twice a week

Go beyond the limit and you are dropped from the course.

  • Be on time and stay until class ends.
  • The class secretary checks attendance every meeting.
  • Catch up on missed lessons in the module; arrange make-up workshops or performances with me.

AAP 101 · Course Syllabus · Classroom Policies21
High-standard academic outputs

Your work, your name, your honesty

✨ Originality matters

Art is personal expression. Every critique, photo, storyboard, painting and performance must be your own idea and your own effort.

🤖 AI use, only when the task allows

  • Allowed for brainstorming and practice only when the task says so, with disclosure.
  • Never in quizzes and exams.
  • AI-generated images can't be submitted as your own artwork.

🚫 Plagiarism will not go unpunished

Copying, cheating, or passing off others' or AI's work as yours is a violation. Violations are dealt with under the University's rules and regulations.

Good habits

Keep your account safe and your progress smooth

✅ Do

  • Keep your access code to yourself.
  • Sign in on any device; your progress follows your code.
  • Sign out when you use a shared or borrowed phone.
  • Finish each week's lesson before class.
  • Check Quizzes & Exams for open and upcoming tests.

⛔ Don't

  • Share or borrow access codes: one code runs one live session, so a second sign-in ends the first.
  • Leave the page during a quiz or exam.
  • Use AI tools or outside help in tests.
  • Rush your name and section at first sign-in.
  • Wait until the last day; tests close on schedule.
